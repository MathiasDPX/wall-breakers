## Userscript

A userscript that displays a banner at the top of compatible websites, with a redirect to Wall Breakers, is available at [`https://news.mathiasd.fr/redirect.user.js`](https://news.mathiasd.fr/redirect.user.js) or [`static/userscript.js`](./static/userscript.js). It is compatible with Tampermonkey, Greasemonkey and probably more
